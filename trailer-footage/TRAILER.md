# NutriQuest — 60-Second Anime Trailer

Everything a video-gen pipeline needs: script, character sheets, shot list with prompts, music direction, timing map.

**Title:** NUTRIQUEST — *Every Meal Is a Battle*
**Runtime:** 60 seconds
**Format:** Classical anime trailer — protagonist-driven, 4-act structure (Ordinary World → Summoning → Trials → Awakening → Title). Heroine is on screen in nearly every shot; the monsters are her party, not the stars.
**Style:** TV-anime trailer. High-saturation 2D cel look, thick outlines, speed lines, sakuga flourishes on action beats. *One Punch Man* opening energy × *Pokémon* journey heart.
**Aspect:** 16:9 primary (crop safe for 9:16 vertical cuts).

---

## Logline

A girl who couldn't stick to anything discovers that every meal she logs summons a monster that fights for her — and the deeper she climbs, the more the casino under the arena wants her to fall.

## Protagonist

**RIN (placeholder name)** — the player, heroine, main character.

- **Age/look:** ~17. Messy dark ponytail, oversized gym hoodie, beat-up sneakers, phone on a cracked lanyard. Band-aid on one knee — she tries things and falls.
- **Archetype:** classic shonen heroine in the *ordinary-girl-who-refuses-to-quit* mold. No powers of her own — her monsters fight, but her *consistency* is the power. The trailer must sell her face, her stubbornness, her grin.
- **Arc in 60s:** tired/quitting → summons first monster → trains, eats, collects → loses, keeps going → walks into the casino's shadow → smiles → charge.
- **Acting note for prompts:** she is never a damsel and never cold. Expressions: exhausted → startled → determined → furious → grinning. The grin is the logo of the trailer.

| **The First Seed** | Secret | Fiber | Final flash — floats above Rin's open palm |

**Element color code (lighting/particles):** Protein = ember orange · Fiber = forest green · Vitamin = gold · Hydration = crystal blue.

**Antagonist (visual only, no lines):** the Casino — a shadowed neon pit with a molten cauldron. Give it presence, not a face.

**House Rule (stakes, established silently):** a challenger's monster that loses in the casino doesn't just faint — it shrinks and hardens into a gold coin, swept into the house's pile. Show this ONCE, small, in the background of S11 (a previous loser's caged monster mid-shrink) — so by S12's "they bet everything I built," the audience already knows what losing costs. No line of dialogue needed, just one visible coin-monster in frame.

## Music

60-second classical anime-OP edit:

- **0–8s:** Solo piano, lonely, kitchen ambience — her ordinary world
- **8s:** Taiko hit + guitar riff — the summon
- **8–40s:** Driving 170 BPM J-rock — drums on every cut, strings stabs on impacts
- **40–52s:** Music drops to half-time for the casino — low choir, tension
- **52–57s:** Full-key change finale, choir swells on the charge
- **57–60s:** Hard silence → logo → one shy piano note on the stinger

**Reference vibe:** "THE HERO!!" (JAM Project) meets "Again" (YUI, FMA:B OP1 — heroine energy).

**Voiceover:** Rin narrates herself — soft-spoken lines delivered like a promise. Optional male trailer-voice alternate exists below.

**SFX beats:** capsule chime 15s · impact boom 33s · coin cascade 40s · meteor flash 55s.

## Shot-by-Shot Sequence (with AI video prompts)

### ACT 1 — ORDINARY WORLD (0:00–0:08) — her, before

**S1 (0:00–0:04)**
- **Cut in:** cold open, no lead-in — hard cut straight to motion
- **Visual:** Alarm blares — Rin's hand slams it dead without opening her eyes. Match-cut: she's already at the kitchen table, shoving an untouched salad across it, gym bag still zipped by the door where it landed yesterday. Two beats, one shot, no static pose.
- **Rin VO:** *"I kept starting over."*
- **Prompt:** `anime girl slamming alarm clock in dim bedroom, match cut to same girl at kitchen table pushing untouched salad bowl away, gym bag unopened by door, quick two-beat morning routine, muted soft light, slice-of-life anime, weary momentum not stillness`

**S2 (0:04–0:08)**
- **Cut in:** hard cut on the push of the salad bowl
- **Visual:** Her phone lights up on the table — a capsule icon pulsing. She looks up, suspicious.
- **Rin VO:** *"And then dinner fought back."*
- **Prompt:** `close-up anime shot of smartphone glowing on kitchen table with pulsing capsule icon, girl's face reflected in screen looking up suspiciously, dramatic rim lighting, mystery`

### ACT 2 — THE SUMMONING (0:08–0:22) — world cracks open

**S3 (0:08–0:12)** — TAIKO HIT, music ignites
- **Cut in:** flash-cut to white, then slam — hardest cut in Act 1/2
- **Visual:** Capsule on her table EXPLODES — Broccoli Bud lands in hero pose on her open palm. She flinches, then can't look away. Bud looks up at her like she's the chosen one.
- **Prompt:** `tiny broccoli knight character exploding out of capsule onto anime girl's open palm, three-point hero landing, dust shockwave, girl's wide eyes behind it, speed lines, high saturation anime action`

**S4 (0:12–0:15)**
- **Cut in:** speed-line wipe off S3's dust shockwave
- **Visual:** Two-panel montage, 1.5s each (down from three — three subjects in 3s reads as a blur; two holds long enough to land): Bean Sprout spars with her chopsticks, taped fists blurring / Spinach Scout collapses mid-pose on her shoulder then springs back up, leaf-cape flopping. *(Water Droplet dropped from this beat — see roster note above; low narrative value for the frame it cost.)*
- **Prompt:** `anime two-panel montage: scrawny bean sprout boxing girl's chopsticks with taped fists; leaf-caped spinach scout collapsing then springing upright on girl's shoulder, comedic warm tone, vibrant, each panel held long enough to read clearly`

**S5 (0:15–0:18)** — capsule chime
- **Cut in:** match cut on Spinach Scout's spring-up motion into Rin's stride
- **Visual:** Rin runs downhill at golden hour, hoodie flapping, Bud in her front pocket — city below her, vast.
- **Rin VO:** *"Every meal. Every rep. Every day."*
- **Prompt:** `anime girl running downhill at golden hour with tiny broccoli monster peeking from her hoodie pocket, city sprawling below, wind in hair, determined expression, cinematic wide shot, makoto shinkai lighting`

**S6 (0:18–0:22)**
- **Cut in:** whip pan from Rin's run to the waterfall
- **Visual:** Salmon Striker rockets up a waterfall — she watches from below, water misting her face, smiling for the first time.
- **Prompt:** `sleek salmon warrior leaping up massive waterfall in slow motion while anime girl watches from below smiling through the spray, low angle, sakuga water animation, dynamic`

### ACT 3 — TRIALS (0:22–0:40) — collect, fuse, fight, fall

**S7 (0:22–0:26)**
- **Cut in:** hard cut on music drop into Act 3
- **Visual:** Gacha machine spins — rainbow light — Rin's face reflected in the glass, eyes tracking silhouettes, holding her breath.
- **Title card:** `COLLECT`
- **Prompt:** `anime close-up of girl's face reflected in glowing gacha machine glass, rainbow light flares, monster silhouettes cycling past her eyes, neon arcade, anticipation`

**S8 (0:26–0:30)**
- **Cut in:** cross-fade through the rainbow light of the gacha glass
- **Visual:** Fusion ritual — three Buds dissolve into light streams around her raised hands; a star ignites above her palm.
- **Title card:** `FUSE`
- **Prompt:** `three identical broccoli creatures dissolving into spiraling light streams above anime girl's raised hands, star igniting above her palm, summoning circle on ground, magical girl transformation energy, particles`

**S9 (0:30–0:33)** — impact boom
- **Cut in:** hard cut, no transition — impact needs zero runway
- **Visual:** Arena — a flaming fist comes straight at RIN; Avocado Aegis slams in front of her at the last frame, shield cracking, shockwave blasting her hair back.
- **Title card:** `BATTLE`
- **Prompt:** `flaming punch flying directly at anime girl's face, round avocado shield-bearer slamming in front of her at last instant, shield cracking, shockwave blasting her ponytail back, anime impact frame, arena crowd silhouettes`

**S10 (0:33–0:37)**
- **Cut in:** slow dissolve from the shockwave frame, tempo drops
- **Visual:** She loses. On one knee in the arena dirt, knuckles on the ground, monsters battered around her. She breathes. She gets up.
- **Rin VO:** *"I lost. Then I logged breakfast."*
- **Prompt:** `anime girl on one knee in battle arena dirt, bruised monsters lying around her, she pushes herself up with shaking arms, dust and dramatic backlight, comeback moment, emotional`

**S11 (0:37–0:40)** — music halves, coin cascade
- **Cut in:** hard cut on her rising from one knee
- **Visual:** Neon escalator descends into the Monster Casino — molten cauldron glow below. Rin steps on, Bud gripping her sleeve. Background, one cage passing at frame edge: a previous challenger's monster mid-shrink into a gold coin — small, half-second, not dwelt on. This is the only place the House Rule is shown before it's spoken.
- **Title card:** `RISK EVERYTHING`
- **Prompt:** `anime girl descending neon escalator into dark casino, giant molten gold cauldron glowing far below, tiny broccoli monster gripping her sleeve, purple and gold neon, background cage at frame edge with a small creature mid-transformation into a gold coin, descending into danger shot`

### ACT 4 — AWAKENING (0:40–0:57) — everything she earned

**S12 (0:40–0:44)**
- **Cut in:** hard cut off the coin-cage detail from S11
- **Visual:** Casino floor — monsters wagered in glowing cages. Pomegranate Paladin and Turmeric Titan stand guard. Rin walks through them, unflinching, eyes forward.
- **Rin VO:** *"They bet everything I built."*
- **Prompt:** `anime girl walking calmly through dark neon casino floor, glowing cages with monsters, ruby paladin and golden titan guarding behind her, she does not flinch, confident stride, dramatic purple-red lighting`

**S13 (0:44–0:49)**
- **Cut in:** hard cut on her final footstep
- **Visual:** Her phone screen: her own monster lineup blazing. She slams it face-up on the casino table. *(Narrowed from "whole room turns" — a crowd-reaction shot is the hardest thing here to generate cleanly and reads as noise, not tension. Instead: one dealer's hand freezes mid-reach for the chips, and the Pomegranate Paladin's eyes narrow behind Rin, just visible over her shoulder. Two subjects, not a room.)*
- **Rin VO:** *"So did I."*
- **Prompt:** `anime girl slamming smartphone face-up onto casino table, screen blazing with monster lineup, dealer's hand frozen mid-reach for chips, ruby paladin's narrowed eyes visible over girl's shoulder in background, dramatic tension shot, low angle`

**S14 (0:49–0:53)**
- **Cut in:** floor-shatter hard cut, no runway — this is the mythic reveal
- **Visual:** Spirulina Wyrm ERUPTS through the casino floor — coils around the moon visible through the shattered skylight, bioluminescent scales raining light onto Rin's upturned grin.
- **Prompt:** `ancient teal serpent erupting through casino floor, coiling around full moon through shattered skylight, bioluminescent scale light raining onto anime girl's upturned grinning face, mythic reveal, epic sakuga`

**S15 (0:53–0:57)** — key change, full choir
- **Cut in:** hard cut on the wyrm's light hitting Rin's face
- **Visual:** THE RUN — Rin at the front of a 14-monster charge straight at camera, hoodie trailing, elements blazing behind her like a comet tail.
- **Rin VO:** *"Every meal is a battle."*
- **Prompt:** `anime girl leading fourteen food monsters charging directly at camera, hero run shot, girl in front with ponytail and hoodie streaming, elemental trails orange green gold blue, speed lines, epic anime finale charge`

### TITLE (0:57–1:00) — hard silence

**S16 (0:57–0:59)**
- **Cut in:** hard silence, hard cut to black — the one true beat of stillness in the whole trailer
- **Visual:** Black → NUTRIQUEST logo slams in. Tagline: *"Every Meal Is a Battle."* — The First Seed pulses once above the logo.
- **Prompt:** `anime title card slamming onto black screen with impact frames, small glowing cyan seed pulsing softly above the logo, dramatic then gentle`

**S17 (0:59–1:00)** — one shy piano note
- **Cut in:** cut on the piano note, logo still up or just faded
- **Visual:** Stinger — Bud tugs Rin's sleeve; she looks down, deadpans: *"…you ate my cereal."*
- **Prompt:** `tiny broccoli knight tugging anime girl's sleeve looking guilty, girl looking down deadpan, comedic anime stinger, soft lighting, end-card moment`

## Production Notes

- **Character lock first:** generate Rin reference sheet (front/side/expressions) before ANY scene — she must be identical across all 17 shots. Then Bud. Everyone else secondary. **A prompt-only ref sheet is not enough** — plain repeated text prompts drift face/hair/proportions shot to shot. Train Rin (and Bud) as a reusable character/identity model (Higgsfield Soul or equivalent) from the locked ref sheet, then generate every shot off that trained identity, not off the prompt text alone.
- **Quality anchors:** S3 (summon), S10 (defeat), S13 (table slam), S15 (the run). Nail these four; the rest can be B-tier.
- **Hard sync points:** 8s, 22s, 40s, 49s, 57s — cuts land on music hits.
- **Vertical cut:** S3, S7, S9, S13, S15, S16 reframe to 9:16 cleanly → TikTok edit.
- **Classical format checklist hit:** cold-open ordinary world ✓ inciting summon ✓ montage growth ✓ low-point defeat ✓ descent to underworld (casino) ✓ awakening reveal ✓ hero charge ✓ title + stinger ✓
- **Font:** heavy condensed sans for title (Ghost if licensed, else Anton); tagline letterspaced medium.
