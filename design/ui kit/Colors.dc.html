ELEVATION — NEW
Every card is a contact shadow + a softer ambient shadow, plus a barely-there inner top highlight — never a flat outline standing in for depth. Three levels, used consistently: E1 for anything resting (grid cards, list rows), E2 for anything raised or asking for attention (a featured card, an active state), E3 for a genuine hero moment. An accent-tinted glow layers on top of any of these for celebratory or "this is yours" moments.

RARITY — 7 TIERS
Extended from 4 to 7 tiers to match the game's real economy (common/uncommon/rare/epic/legendary/mythic/secret). Uncommon reuses the brand's own link-hover green rather than inventing a new hue; mythic is new — a rose-crimson chosen to sit clearly apart from epic's purple and legendary's gold. Secret is the one holographic ring in the whole system, reserved for the single rarest tier so it reads as a genuine event rather than decoration.
