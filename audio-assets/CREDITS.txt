Format note: OGG Vorbis. iOS AVAudioPlayer does not decode OGG — converted to
.caf via `afconvert -f caff -d aac -b 128000 in.ogg out.caf`.
The .caf set is bundled in the app at ios/Sources/NutriQuestUI/Resources/Audio/
and played by NQSound (ios/Sources/NutriQuestUI/NQSound.swift).
