Point the camera at any food barcode — EAN, UPC, whatever's in the pantry. Nutrition, ingredients, and origin pulled live from Open Food Facts. Free, unlimited.

Protein, fiber, and quality bars track today's nutrition. Hit your targets and your squad earns a daily multiplier boost in battle.

Apple Watch steps, heart rate, and workouts feed your Daily Boost — up to +10% battle power for staying active.
